Hook — Support

Hook is built and maintained by Marion Moranetz. Email is the fastest way to get a response.

Common questions

  • Lost data after reinstall? Hook is local-first. Use iCloud sync (if enabled in Settings) before deleting the app to preserve data across reinstalls.
  • Apple Health integration not working? Open the iOS Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → Hook → confirm read/write permissions are enabled for the categories the app needs.
  • Bug or feature request? Email me with the iOS version, the device model, and steps to reproduce.
