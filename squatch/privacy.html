Squatch privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026. This covers Squatch 1.2.

What stays on your phone

Your food log, weigh-ins, goals, fullness and hunger entries, squats and settings are stored in a database on your device. If iCloud is on, that database syncs through your own private iCloud account so your devices agree. It sits in your Apple account; I cannot read it.

The food database ships inside the app. Searching for a food by name runs on your phone and sends nothing anywhere.

The one thing that leaves your phone

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is looked up on Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), an open food database. Only that number is sent: no image, no account, no device identifier, none of your logs or health data. Open Food Facts has its own privacy policy for the requests it receives.

Camera

The camera is used only to read a barcode when you tap Scan barcode. No photo is taken or stored.

Apple Health

Apple Health is off until you turn it on in Camp and grant permission. When on, Squatch reads the categories you allow (such as weight, steps, active energy, nutrition and, where available, blood glucose and sleep) and writes back the foods and weigh-ins you log. Health data moves between the app and Apple Health on the device and is never sent anywhere else. You can revoke access in Settings, Privacy & Security, Health.

What Squatch does not do

No account or login. No analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising, and no third-party SDKs are bundled in the app. No tracking across apps or websites. No location. Nothing is sold or shared. There are no in-app purchases.

Importing

A CSV export from another tracker is read on your device and written to your local database. The file is not transmitted.

Deleting your data

Open Camp and tap Erase everything. It clears every log and weigh-in on the device and the iCloud-synced copy with it. Deleting the app also removes the data on that device.
