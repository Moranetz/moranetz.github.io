Squatch

A calorie and weight tracker that works out what you actually burn from your own logs and scale, and draws the day as a climb up a mountain.

How is my burn worked out?

Squatch waits until it has 3 logged days and 2 weigh-ins 5 days apart, then estimates your maintenance by energy balance: your 14-day average intake plus the deficit implied by the trend of your weigh-ins. Until then the number reads as a dash rather than a guess. These are estimates for healthy adults; talk to a clinician about any medical situation.

Why does my weight look smoother than the scale?

Daily weight swings with water and salt, so Squatch shows a moving-average trend through your weigh-ins. The trend is the part that moves with fat.

Does search need a signal?

No. The food database is on your phone. Only barcode scanning looks a number up online.

How do I delete all my data?

Open Camp and tap Erase everything. It clears your logs and weigh-ins on the device and in your iCloud copy, and keeps the plan you set. Anything Squatch wrote to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

Is there anything to buy?

No. Squatch is free, with no subscription and no ads.
