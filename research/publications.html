Entry 01
Title Filters Don’t Work. Palettes Do.
Publisher moranetz.github.io — The Measurement Layer
Date August 2026
URL https://moranetz.github.io/research/melanopic/
PDF moranetz-2026-filters-dont-work-palettes-do.pdf
Description

"Night mode" is a marketing claim, not a measurement. I built a per-pixel melanopic-dose instrument and pointed it at the interfaces people trust before bed.

The counterintuitive result is Kindle. Its sepia mode doses at 76% of white. It's warm and it's bright, and luminance is what does the damage, so warmth is a decoy.

The second result kills the obvious fix. A red filter strong enough to cut melanopic dose 21x collapses body-text contrast to APCA Lc 9.7. One gentle enough to stay readable cuts dose by only 1.5x. There's nothing usable in between, because a filter treats glyphs and fields identically. A palette pushes the field down and holds the type up.

Instruments are open source. Reported as a relative figure on one modeled panel, never as lux, because the model can't produce those units.

Entry 02
Title Measured Neuroaesthetics
Publisher moranetz.github.io — The Measurement Layer
Date September 2026
URL https://moranetz.github.io/research/neuroaesthetics/
PDF moranetz-2026-measured-neuroaesthetics.pdf
Description

"This interface feels premium" gets treated as ineffable taste. It decomposes into eight mechanisms, each tied to a property you can already instrument: contrast, symmetry, curvature, CIELAB colour coordinates, complexity, and a behavioral liking-wanting signature.

The organizing claim is the one mechanism that isn't a static pixel property. Reward splits into hedonic liking and incentive wanting, and the two come apart. Good design keeps them aligned. A dark pattern engineers wanting past liking. Aesthetic quality and manipulation turn out to be the same measurement read in opposite directions.

The paper also quarantines the folklore design writing repeats: the golden ratio as a beauty law, the Mehrabian 7-38-55 figures applied outside their original scope, and rigid cross-cultural colour universals.

Every citation carries a grade on the page. Running that gate found two real defects in my own draft. One attribution resolved to no existing paper, and three references were never cited in the body at all. Both are recorded in the paper rather than deleted.

Entry 03
Title The Unmeasured Half of the Neural Interface
Publisher moranetz.github.io — The Measurement Layer
Date September 2026
URL https://moranetz.github.io/research/neural-interface-ux/
PDF moranetz-2026-unmeasured-half-neural-interface.pdf
Description

Brain-computer interface research has spent two decades optimizing the decoder, measured almost entirely by Information Transfer Rate. ITR has no term for user effort, fatigue, dropout, or subjective experience. A system can post a high number and be exhausting to use for ten minutes.

Meanwhile 15 to 30% of users can't reliably control a given motor-imagery BCI no matter how good the decoder is. Part of that is an interface artifact rather than a biological ceiling, which makes it a design failure at population scale instead of a user failure.

The argument: the other half of a neural interface is how it shows you its read of you, and whether you can calibrate trust in a system inferring your own internal state. That's a measurement problem a behavioral scientist is positioned to own. Six instrumentable open problems follow.

One company in the draft couldn't be verified. It's printed as unverifiable rather than described.

Entry 04
Title The Evolved Substrate of Human Influenceability
Publisher moranetz.github.io — The Measurement Layer
Date September 2026
URL https://moranetz.github.io/research/evolved-substrate/
PDF moranetz-2026-evolved-substrate-influenceability.pdf
Description

Modern persuasion doesn't defeat your reasoning. It goes around it, by showing evolved motivational systems stimuli those systems never met in the environment that shaped them.

Niko Tinbergen's herring gulls pecked hardest at a red knitting needle with three white bands, not at an accurate model of a parent's head. He called it a supernormal stimulus. This paper argues the digital attention economy is that, industrialised, and organises the exploitable surface into six evolved systems: reward tuned to unpredictable foraging returns, error-management bias, coalitional psychology, reciprocity, attachment, and status signalling.

The payoff is defensive. Naming the evolved system under a technique turns "this feels manipulative" into a question you can answer: which system is being triggered, and is the trigger honest? Is the uncertainty real content, or a server withholding an outcome? Is the scarcity real inventory?

Running a primary-source pass before publishing caught three errors in my own draft. I had collapsed two distinct dopamine responses reported by Fiorillo, Tobler and Schultz in Science 2003, where the phasic burst tracks probability monotonically and a separate sustained ramp is the one that peaks at p = 0.5. I had credited coalition detection to the wrong Cosmides and Tooby paper; it belongs to Kurzban, Tooby and Cosmides, PNAS 2001. And I had Katiyar et al. 2025 under Psychological Bulletin when it ran in Psychological Review, with an author missing. All three are corrected in the text and labelled as corrections rather than swapped out.

The paper also quarantines what discredits this literature: the triune "lizard brain," alpha-wolf dominance, and just-so storytelling. L. David Mech spent thirteen summers with wild wolves on Ellesmere Island and has been asking people to drop the alpha framing since 1999.
