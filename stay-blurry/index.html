A Squatch × WildHearts crossover

Stay
Blurry

Squatch lives in a moonlit forest, and the camera drones have found him. Each one wants a single clear photo. Shoot them down with a laser, hide in the pines, and keep the legend a smudge.

Help

Drag on the left side of the screen to move and on the right side to aim and fire. Stand under a tree or in a berry bush and the drones lose you. Your progress, records and Field Book live on your device; deleting the app deletes them.
