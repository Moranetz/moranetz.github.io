Stay Blurry privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026.

What the game collects

Nothing. Stay Blurry has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no network requests. It runs entirely on your device.

What stays on your device

Your best scores, unlocked hats and perks, Field Book entries and the polaroids the drones took are saved on your device so they are there next time. The web version at this address also offers an optional decision log, off unless you turn it on, saved the same way in your browser. None of it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Photos

If you choose to share or save a polaroid, iOS asks for your permission first, and the image goes only where you send it.
