SLOP OPS — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-08-26 · Developer: Marion Moranetz

SLOP OPS is local-first and makes no network calls. What you do in the app stays on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK. Nothing is transmitted anywhere.

What is collected

Nothing.

SLOP OPS stores its data — your progress and settings — locally on your device using iOS’s standard UserDefaults. This data:

  • Never leaves your device — the app makes no network requests, and there is no server to send anything to
  • Is not transmitted to me or any third party
  • Is encrypted by iOS using your device passcode and the iOS Data Protection class

What the app never touches

No network. No account or sign-in. No iCloud sync. No Apple Health. No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo-library access. No push notifications.

Third-party services

SLOP OPS does not use third-party analytics (Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment), advertising SDKs (AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, Unity Ads), off-device crash reporters (Sentry, Crashlytics, Bugsnag), or tracking SDKs. If this ever changes, this policy and the app's release notes will say so.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

SLOP OPS is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone. If you have a concern, contact me at the address below.

Your rights

  • Delete your data: delete the app, or use any in-app reset/clear option.
  • Export your data: if the app supports export, it's in Settings; if not, contact me.
  • Stop tracking: there is nothing to stop — SLOP OPS does not track you.

For users under GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, or similar: because no personal data is collected on any server I control, there is no controller-side data to access, port, or erase. Local data is governed by Apple's iOS standards and your control of your device.

Changes

Updates get a new "Effective" date here and a note in the next App Store release notes.
