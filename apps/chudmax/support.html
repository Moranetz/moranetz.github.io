Common questions

"It didn't spot a face."

Heavy profiles and covered faces can slip past the spotter. Tap + Drop a cover and drag it into place — it works exactly like a spotted one. The export check will still verify the result.

"How do I add my own sticker?"

Any transparent PNG works. Tap Add on the sticker shelf and pick it from your photos. It stays on the shelf for every photo and clip after.

"Is my photo uploaded anywhere?"

No. Everything runs on your phone. See the privacy policy.

"I paid for Pro on my old phone."

Open any video, reach the unlock screen, and tap Restore purchases. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID.

"I want a refund."

App Store refunds go through Apple, not me: reportaproblem.apple.com

"The tag on my print."

The Chudmax tag is on by default and one tap turns it off — the toggle is right on the export screen, above Save.
