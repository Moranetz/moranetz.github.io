Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the Settings app, tap your Apple ID at the top, then Subscriptions, then CalTracker, then Cancel Subscription.

How do I delete all my data?
In the app, go to Profile, then Preferences, then Delete All Data. This wipes the local store and the iCloud-synced copy.

How are calorie targets computed?
We use Mifflin-St Jeor for resting metabolic rate, multiply by an activity factor, then adjust by your goal rate at the standard ~7,700 kcal per kg conversion. The minimum is clamped at 1,200 kcal as a safety floor. These are estimates for healthy adults; talk to a clinician for any medical situation.

Is my food data shared?
No. Food entries, weigh-ins, goals, and onboarding answers stay on your device (and your private iCloud account if iCloud is enabled). See the Privacy Policy for the full list.
