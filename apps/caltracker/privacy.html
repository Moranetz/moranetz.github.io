Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-23

CalTracker (the "App", "we") is built to keep your nutrition data on your device and under your control. This page explains exactly what is collected, what stays local, and what (if anything) is sent off-device.

What stays on your device only

  • Your food log entries, weigh-ins, goals, and onboarding answers. All of it is stored in a local SwiftData container.
  • Your iCloud-synced copy, if you have iCloud enabled, lives in your private iCloud account. We cannot read it.
  • Your Apple Health reads and writes, governed by the permissions you grant Apple Health directly.

What may be sent off-device

  • Subscription state: Apple's StoreKit handles your purchase and tells the App whether you have an active subscription. We do not store your payment information.
  • Food-scan images (optional): if you use the camera-scan feature and a third-party AI provider is configured, the image is sent to that provider for recognition and discarded after the response returns. The provider's own privacy policy applies during that round-trip.
  • Anonymous product analytics (optional): if you have not opted out in Settings, the App reports anonymous event counts (screens viewed, features used) so we can fix the rough edges. No food entries, weights, or personal identifiers are included.
  • Crash reports (optional): if you have not opted out, the App sends crash stack traces to help us fix issues.

What is never collected

  • Your name, email, phone, address, or contacts.
  • Your photo library beyond images you explicitly capture for food scanning.
  • Your location.
  • Advertising identifiers.

Data deletion

To delete every byte the App holds locally, tap Profile → Preferences → Delete All Data. The local SwiftData store, your iCloud-synced copy (if any), and the keychain entries are wiped. Apple Health entries you previously authorized stay in Apple Health itself; revoke access from Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Children

CalTracker is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy materially changes we will update the "Last updated" date above and surface a notice in the App.
