Health and safety disclaimer

Squatch's targets are computed from population-average formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor and Harris-Benedict variants for resting metabolism, plus an activity multiplier). They are estimates for healthy adults. They are not appropriate for pregnancy, lactation, eating-disorder recovery, or any medical condition that affects metabolism. If any of those apply to you, work with a clinician and treat Squatch's numbers as suggestions only.
