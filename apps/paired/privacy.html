Paired Attachment Dating — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-08-26 · Developer: Marion Moranetz

Paired Attachment Dating has no backend. Your profile, preferences, and assessment answers are stored in local files on your device and are never transmitted to me or any server I control. Sign-in is handled by Apple; the optional calendar feature writes events only with your permission. No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK.

What is collected

Stored on your device, sent nowhere.

Paired stores the data you provide — your email, profile details, private preferences, and your attachment-assessment answers — in local files on your device, excluded from device backups. This data:

  • Never leaves your device — the app has no server, and nothing you enter is transmitted to me or any third party
  • Is encrypted by iOS using your device passcode and the iOS Data Protection class

Sign in with Apple

Authentication uses Apple’s own Sign in with Apple. Apple handles the sign-in; the app receives your Apple ID credential (and your email if you choose to share it) and stores it locally on the device only. No credential is sent to any server of mine — there isn’t one.

Calendar (optional)

If you use the scheduling feature, the app asks for write-only calendar access and adds the event you created to your calendar via Apple’s EventKit. It does not read your existing calendar events.

What the app never touches

No network requests. No iCloud sync. No Apple Health. No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo access. No push notifications.

Third-party services

Paired Attachment Dating does not use third-party analytics (Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment), advertising SDKs (AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, Unity Ads), off-device crash reporters (Sentry, Crashlytics, Bugsnag), or tracking SDKs. If this ever changes, this policy and the app's release notes will say so.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Paired Attachment Dating is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone. If you have a concern, contact me at the address below.

Your rights

  • Delete your data: delete the app, or use any in-app reset/clear option.
  • Export your data: if the app supports export, it's in Settings; if not, contact me.
  • Stop tracking: there is nothing to stop — Paired Attachment Dating does not track you.

For users under GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, or similar: because no personal data is collected on any server I control, there is no controller-side data to access, port, or erase. Local data is governed by Apple's iOS standards and your control of your device.

Changes

Updates get a new "Effective" date here and a note in the next App Store release notes.
