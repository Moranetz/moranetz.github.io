Whose Round — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-08-26 · Developer: Marion Moranetz

Whose Round keeps your groups, rounds, and history on your device and makes no network calls of its own. The one time data goes anywhere is when you tap settle: the payment handle, amount, and note you chose are placed in a link that opens Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal. No account, no server, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK.

What is collected

Stored on your device, sent nowhere.

Whose Round stores your friends, groups, rounds, and settle history locally on your device. This data:

  • Is not transmitted to me or any server — the app has none
  • Is encrypted by iOS using your device passcode and the iOS Data Protection class

Adding friends from Contacts

If you add a friend from Contacts, the app uses Apple’s out-of-process contact picker: it never gets access to your address book, only to the one contact you pick, and it keeps just that contact’s name and photo, locally.

The one place data goes: settling up

When you tap settle, the app builds a link containing the recipient’s payment handle, the amount, and (for Venmo) the note, and hands it to the payment app you chose — Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal. From that moment, that company’s privacy policy governs what happens; nothing comes back to Whose Round. If you never tap settle, no data goes anywhere at all.

What the app never touches

No network requests of its own. No account or sign-in. No iCloud sync. No Apple Health. No location, camera, or microphone. No push notifications.

Third-party services

Whose Round does not use third-party analytics (Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment), advertising SDKs (AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, Unity Ads), off-device crash reporters (Sentry, Crashlytics, Bugsnag), or tracking SDKs. If this ever changes, this policy and the app's release notes will say so.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Whose Round is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone. If you have a concern, contact me at the address below.

Your rights

  • Delete your data: delete the app, or use any in-app reset/clear option.
  • Export your data: if the app supports export, it's in Settings; if not, contact me.
  • Stop tracking: there is nothing to stop — Whose Round does not track you.

For users under GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, or similar: because no personal data is collected on any server I control, there is no controller-side data to access, port, or erase. Local data is governed by Apple's iOS standards and your control of your device.

Changes

Updates get a new "Effective" date here and a note in the next App Store release notes.
